About the Course

Mathematics at technical universities is an organic part of study. It should not be perceived as the final goal, but as an essential tool for understanding professional subjects. The aim of this course is to teach students not only basic mathematical concepts, procedures, and methods, but also to develop their logical thinking. Students should learn how to analyze problems, distinguish the essential from the non-essential, propose methods of solving, verify each step, generalize conclusions, evaluate correctness based on given conditions, and apply mathematical tasks to solve technical problems. The course also shows that mathematical methods and logical reasoning are used far beyond mathematics itself.
